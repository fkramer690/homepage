Based on the October 2026 price list, Agentforce 1 Field Service at $650 per user per month paid off only in a narrow case: when the company would have bought Slack, Tableau Next, Digital Channels and Data 360 for the same users anyway. Built from individual components, Field Service Plus with the Agentforce, Visual Remote Assistant and Appointment Assistant add-ons costs $430, so the bundle costs $220 more per user per month. The included credits at $5,000 list value cover that premium for about two users. The US list now shows Field Service Max at $650 instead; the method below carries over, but the result does not.

```hinweis achtung
On 5 November 2026 the US [Field Service pricing page](https://www.salesforce.com/service/field-service-management/pricing/) replaced Agentforce 1 Field Service with Field Service Max at the same $650, now with 2.75 million Flex Credits per org and year, Visual Remote Assistant, Asset Service Lifecycle Management and Connected Assets, and Field Service Plus with Field Service+ Core at $250. The German page still showed the old model on 9 November 2026. The calculation below uses the October price list; the method stays the same, but redo it with the figures in your quote.
```

## What is in Agentforce 1 Field Service?

Salesforce uses the name Field Service and Operations in parts of its pricing pages. In October 2026, the [Field Service pricing page](https://www.salesforce.com/service/field-service-management/pricing/) listed Agentforce 1 Field Service at $650 per user per month, on an annual contract and for Enterprise Edition. The [German page](https://www.salesforce.com/de/products/field-service/pricing) shows €650. According to the price list, the bundle contains:

- everything in Field Service Plus, meaning Dispatcher, Technician and the right to use case management,
- unlimited Agentforce use for employees,
- Appointment Assistant, Visual Remote Assistant, Digital Channels, Slack and Tableau Next,
- 1 million Flex Credits and 2.5 million Data Cloud credits (now Data 360) per org per year.

```hinweis offen
In the feature grid, the row for the Scheduling Agent says "1M Flex Credits included per org" for this edition and "Requires Flex Credits" for all others. Whether that is a second million or the same pool as above, the page does not say. I would have it confirmed in the quote.
```

A second detail: in October the general [Agentforce pricing page](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/) mentioned Agentforce 1 editions "from $550" with 2.5 million Flex Credits per org per year; on 9 November it shows Max Edition from $550 with 2.75 million. The Field Service page says 1 million Flex Credits and 2.5 million Data Cloud credits. For the calculation I use the Field Service page and would ask for the exact figures in the order form.

## From single parts, the core costs $430

The same pricing page lists the add-ons separately. Per user per month, at list price:

| Part | List price |
|---|---|
| Field Service Plus | $230 |
| Agentforce for Field Service and Operations | $125 |
| [Visual Remote Assistant](../visual-remote-assistant-remote-help-instead-of-visit/) | $50 |
| Appointment Assistant | $25 |
| **Sum** | **$430** |
| Agentforce 1 Field Service | $650 |
| **Difference** | **$220** |

So the sum covers four components, not the whole bundle: Slack, Tableau Next, Digital Channels and the Data 360 credits are not included on the left side. For these, I have no list price from the Field Service page, so you need to include them in your own quote. The question is simple: would you otherwise pay more than $220 per user per month for them?

The Agentforce add-on already includes unmetered use for employees. The Agentforce pricing page says this generally for the $125 add-ons. Unmetered employee use alone therefore does not justify the bundle premium.

## What are the included credits worth?

Salesforce lists Flex Credits at [$500 per 100,000](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/), and a standard action costs 20 credits, according to the [announcement of the pricing model](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2025/05/15/agentforce-flexible-pricing-news/). One million Flex Credits are therefore worth at most $5,000 a year at list price, or 50,000 standard actions, but only if you would otherwise have bought them. If the Scheduling Agent row means a second million, it is $10,000.

The premium is per user, the credits are per org. $220 per user per month is $2,640 per user per year. $5,000 in credits cover the premium for 1.9 users, $10,000 for 3.8 users. Starting with the fifth user, the credits alone never justify the bundle. How to estimate credits per process in detail is covered in [Agentforce costs for manufacturers](../flex-credits-agentforce-cost-per-action/).

## Worked example: 50 users and a booking agent

All numbers are assumptions. A manufacturer of compressors has 50 Field Service users, 40 technicians and 10 people in dispatch and hotline. A Scheduling Agent books 1,500 maintenance visits a month with customers. I assume 5 actions per booking: identify the customer, find the asset, look for slots, book, confirm. That is 100 credits per booking, a simplified scenario: prompts, Data 360 and sandbox tests are not included.

**Credit requirement.** 1,500 bookings times 100 credits equals 150,000 credits a month, or 1.8 million a year. At list price that is $9,000 a year.

**Option A: everyone on Agentforce 1 Field Service.** 50 times $650 is $32,500 a month, $390,000 a year. The included credits cover 1 million, or all 1.8 million if the Scheduling Agent row means a second million.

**Option B: everyone on individual components.** 50 times $430 is $21,500 a month, or $258,000 a year. Add 1.8 million credits for $9,000. That brings the total to $267,000. The bundle costs at least $123,000 more a year.

**Option C: by role.** Technicians get the Technician license, the Agentforce add-on and Appointment Assistant: $325 per month. Giving every user the add-on and every technician Appointment Assistant is a conservative assumption; fewer add-ons widen the gap. Dispatch and hotline staff get Field Service Plus, the add-on and Visual Remote Assistant: $405. That is $13,000 plus $4,050, or $17,050 a month and $204,600 a year, plus $9,000 in credits. The bundle costs at least $176,400 more a year than this mix.

So the bundle pays off in this example only if Slack, Tableau Next, Digital Channels and Data 360 for 50 users would otherwise cost more than $123,000 a year. Whether Salesforce lets you mix the bundle with individual licenses in one contract is a question for your account team; for a role-based mix like option C, I would ask before requesting the first quote.

## When does the bundle pay off, and when do single add-ons win?

```pro-contra
pro: The bundle fits when
- Slack and Tableau Next are already planned for the service organization and would be bought separately
- Data 360 is needed for the use case, for example to ground agents on manuals and service history
- the user group is small and the customer-facing agents use up the included credits
- the users need Field Service Plus anyway, because they work cases and dispatch
contra: Single add-ons win when
- most users are technicians who need Technician plus the $125 add-on
- the company already has Slack or a reporting tool
- customer-facing agents are still in a pilot phase and the credit requirement is unknown
```

A third route falls outside both lists: the Agentic Enterprise License Agreement (AELA), a fixed fee for consumption-based services whose exact scope depends on the contract. When that is worth it is covered in [Salesforce AELA vs Flex Credits](../flex-credits-agentforce-cost-per-action/). Which agents for customers, dispatchers and technicians are ready today is covered in [AI agents in Field Service: what works today?](../field-service-ai-agents-what-works-today/).

## What changes with Field Service Max?

Field Service Max costs the same $650, but the basis for comparison has changed. Field Service+ Advanced costs $450 on the US page, and the [US AI pricing page](https://www.salesforce.com/service/field-service-management/ai/pricing/) lists Agentforce for Field Service at $150. These two parts alone come to $600, before Visual Remote Assistant, Asset Service Lifecycle Management and Connected Assets, which Max includes along with 2.75 million Flex Credits per org per year. The $220 gap of the October bundle therefore does not carry over. I would have the quote confirm whether Field Service+ Advanced already includes any of these parts and which org prerequisites apply before comparing the columns again.
