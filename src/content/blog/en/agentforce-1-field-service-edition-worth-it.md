On the October 2026 price list, Agentforce 1 Field Service at $650 per user and month paid off only in a narrow case: when the company would buy Slack, Tableau Next, Digital Channels and Data 360 for the same users anyway. Built from single parts, Field Service Plus with the Agentforce, Visual Remote Assistant and Appointment Assistant add-ons costs $430, so the bundle costs $220 more per user and month. The included credits at $5,000 list value cover that premium for about two users. The US list now sells Field Service Max at $650 instead; the method below carries over, the result does not.

```hinweis achtung
On 5 November 2026 the US [Field Service pricing page](https://www.salesforce.com/service/field-service-management/pricing/) replaced Agentforce 1 Field Service with Field Service Max at the same $650, now with 2.75 million Flex Credits per org and year, Visual Remote Assistant, Asset Service Lifecycle Management and Connected Assets, and Field Service Plus with Field Service+ Core at $250. The German page still showed the old model on 9 November 2026. The calculation below uses the October price list; the method stays the same, but redo it with the figures in your quote.
```

## What is in Agentforce 1 Field Service?

Salesforce uses the name Field Service and Operations on parts of its pricing pages. The [Field Service pricing page](https://www.salesforce.com/service/field-service-management/pricing/) lists Agentforce 1 Field Service at $650 per user and month in October 2026, on an annual contract and for Enterprise Edition. The [German page](https://www.salesforce.com/de/products/field-service/pricing) shows €650. According to the price list, the bundle contains:

- everything in Field Service Plus, so Dispatcher, Technician and the right to do case management,
- unlimited Agentforce use for employees,
- Appointment Assistant, Visual Remote Assistant, Digital Channels, Slack and Tableau Next,
- 1 million Flex Credits and 2.5 million Data Cloud credits (now Data 360) per org every year.

```hinweis offen
In the feature grid, the row for the Scheduling Agent says "1M Flex Credits included per org" for this edition and "Requires Flex Credits" for all others. Whether that is a second million or the same pool as above, the page does not say. I would have it confirmed in the quote.
```

A second detail: in October the general [Agentforce pricing page](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/) mentioned Agentforce 1 editions "from $550" with 2.5 million Flex Credits per org and year; on 9 November it shows Max Edition from $550 with 2.75 million. The Field Service page says 1 million Flex Credits and 2.5 million Data Cloud credits. For the calculation I use the Field Service page and would ask for the exact figures in the order form.

## From single parts, the core costs $430

The same pricing page lists the add-ons separately. Per user and month, at list price:

| Part | List price |
|---|---|
| Field Service Plus | $230 |
| Agentforce for Field Service and Operations | $125 |
| [Visual Remote Assistant](../visual-remote-assistant-remote-help-instead-of-visit/) | $50 |
| Appointment Assistant | $25 |
| **Sum** | **$430** |
| Agentforce 1 Field Service | $650 |
| **Difference** | **$220** |

So the sum covers four components, not the whole bundle: Slack, Tableau Next, Digital Channels and the Data 360 credits are missing from the left side. For these I have no list price from the Field Service page, so they belong in your own quote. The question is simple: would you pay more than $220 per user and month for them anyway?

The Agentforce add-on already includes unmetered use for employees. The Agentforce pricing page says this for the $125 add-ons in general. Unmetered employee use alone therefore does not justify the bundle premium.

## What are the included credits worth?

Salesforce lists Flex Credits at [$500 per 100,000](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/), and a standard action costs 20 credits, according to the [announcement of the pricing model](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2025/05/15/agentforce-flexible-pricing-news/). One million Flex Credits are therefore worth at most $5,000 a year at list price, or 50,000 standard actions, and only if you would otherwise have bought them. If the Scheduling Agent row means a second million, it is $10,000.

The premium is per user, the credits are per org. $220 per user and month are $2,640 per user and year. $5,000 in credits cover the premium for 1.9 users, $10,000 for 3.8 users. From the fifth user on, the credits alone never justify the bundle. How to estimate credits per process in detail is covered in [Agentforce costs for manufacturers](../flex-credits-agentforce-cost-per-action/).

## Worked example: 50 users and a booking agent

All numbers are assumptions. A manufacturer of compressors has 50 Field Service users, 40 technicians and 10 people in dispatch and hotline. A Scheduling Agent books 1,500 maintenance visits a month with customers. I assume 5 actions per booking: identify the customer, find the asset, look for slots, book, confirm. That is 100 credits per booking, a simplified scenario: prompts, Data 360 and sandbox tests are not included.

**Credit need.** 1,500 bookings times 100 credits are 150,000 credits a month, 1.8 million a year. At list price that is $9,000 a year.

**Option A: everyone on Agentforce 1 Field Service.** 50 times $650 is $32,500 a month, $390,000 a year. The included credits cover 1 million, or all 1.8 million if the Scheduling Agent row means a second million.

**Option B: everyone on parts.** 50 times $430 is $21,500 a month, $258,000 a year. Plus 1.8 million credits for $9,000. Total $267,000. The bundle costs at least $123,000 more a year.

**Option C: by role.** Technicians get Technician, the Agentforce add-on and Appointment Assistant: $325 per month. Giving every user the add-on and every technician Appointment Assistant is a conservative assumption; fewer add-ons widen the gap. Dispatch and hotline get Field Service Plus, the add-on and Visual Remote Assistant: $405. That is $13,000 plus $4,050, so $17,050 a month or $204,600 a year, plus $9,000 in credits. The bundle costs at least $176,400 more a year than this mix.

So the bundle pays off in this example only if Slack, Tableau Next, Digital Channels and Data 360 for 50 users would otherwise cost more than $123,000 a year. Whether Salesforce lets you mix the bundle with single licenses in one contract is a question for your account team; for a role-based mix like option C, I would ask before the first quote.

## When does the bundle pay off, and when do single add-ons win?

```pro-contra
pro: The bundle fits when
- Slack and Tableau Next are already planned for the service organization and would be bought separately
- Data 360 is needed for the use case, for example to ground agents on manuals and service history
- the user group is small and the customer-facing agents use up the included credits
- the users need Field Service Plus anyway, because they work cases and dispatch
contra: Single add-ons win when
- most users are technicians who need Technician plus the $125 add-on
- the company already has Slack or a reporting tool
- customer-facing agents are still a pilot and the credit need is unknown
```

A third route sits outside both lists: the Agentic Enterprise License Agreement (AELA), a fixed fee for consumption services whose exact scope depends on the contract. When that is worth it is covered in [Salesforce AELA vs Flex Credits](../flex-credits-agentforce-cost-per-action/). Which agents for customers, dispatchers and technicians are ready today is covered in [AI agents in Field Service: what works today?](../field-service-ai-agents-what-works-today/).

## What changes with Field Service Max?

Field Service Max costs the same $650, but the comparison side has moved. Field Service+ Advanced costs $450 on the US page, and the [US AI pricing page](https://www.salesforce.com/service/field-service-management/ai/pricing/) lists Agentforce for Field Service at $150. These two parts alone come to $600, before Visual Remote Assistant, Asset Service Lifecycle Management and Connected Assets, which Max includes along with 2.75 million Flex Credits per org and year. The $220 gap of the October bundle therefore does not carry over. Whether Field Service+ Advanced already contains parts of this, and which org prerequisites apply, I would have confirmed in the quote before comparing the columns again.
